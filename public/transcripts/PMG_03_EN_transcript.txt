Interchange and Processing Fees

INTRO
Welcome to Partners Merchant Group Training Academy. Today: Interchange and Processing Fees.

SCENE 1
A card acceptance cost often includes interchange paid through to the issuing bank, network assessments, and a provider’s processing or service charges. The exact statement format varies.

SCENE 2
Card type, transaction type, industry, rewards programs, and acceptance method can affect costs. A keyed sale may have different economics from a chip or contactless transaction.

SCENE 3
Statements may contain per-transaction fees, monthly software charges, gateway fees, PCI-related charges, equipment costs, and other approved items. Read the complete proposal before claiming a savings amount.

SCENE 4
Compare total eligible processing costs to total card volume for the same period. Separate card processing from taxes, loans, and unrelated charges. Explain assumptions and avoid comparing unlike months.

REVIEW
Knowledge check. Can one advertised rate describe every transaction cost?. Answer: Usually not. Review the full statement.

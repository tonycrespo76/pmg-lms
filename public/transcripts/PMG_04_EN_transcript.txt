Pricing Models and Disclosures

INTRO
Welcome to Partners Merchant Group Training Academy. Today: Pricing Models and Disclosures.

SCENE 1
Interchange-plus shows underlying network-related costs and a provider markup. Flat rate uses a simpler published rate. Other programs may use tiered pricing. Each has tradeoffs.

SCENE 2
A disclosed cash-versus-card price program is different from adding a credit card surcharge at checkout. The program must be reviewed for applicable laws, network rules, receipt settings, and signage before activation.

SCENE 3
Credit card surcharging has strict conditions. Do not surcharge debit or prepaid cards, even when the debit card is run without a PIN. Exact requirements depend on the network, location, and program.

SCENE 4
Do not improvise compliance signs, percentage caps, or merchant claims. Use current approved PMG disclosures and obtain a compliance review before launching a pricing program.

REVIEW
Knowledge check. Can a debit card carry a credit-card surcharge?. Answer: No.

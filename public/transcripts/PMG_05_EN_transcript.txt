PARTNERS MERCHANT GROUP TRAINING ACADEMY
Reading a Merchant Statement

INTRO
Welcome to Partners Merchant Group Training Academy. Today: Reading a Merchant Statement.

SCENE 1
Confirm the statement period, merchant location, card sales volume, number of transactions, refunds, chargebacks, and total processing-related charges.

SCENE 2
Divide the relevant total processing fees by card sales volume, then multiply by one hundred. This is an overview, not a substitute for understanding every fee.

SCENE 3
Look for monthly platform charges, equipment rental, gateway services, batch fees, chargeback fees, and minimums. Some charges do not change just because processing volume changes.

SCENE 4
Use matching months, include proposed fixed fees, identify any equipment and contract costs, and get approval before stating savings. Never request full card numbers or sensitive customer data.

REVIEW
Knowledge check. What is the starting point for effective rate?. Answer: Processing fees divided by card sales volume.

PARTNERS MERCHANT GROUP TRAINING ACADEMY
Approaching Business Owners

INTRO
Welcome to Partners Merchant Group Training Academy. Today: Approaching Business Owners.

SCENE 1
Research the business type, busy hours, and likely payment needs. Restaurants need a different conversation from a service business or food truck.

SCENE 2
Introduce yourself briefly: I’m with Partners Merchant Group. Who helps you review payment processing and point-of-sale needs? Ask permission to continue.

SCENE 3
Avoid peak service periods and avoid interrupting staff. If the decision maker is busy, request a better time and confirm the preferred contact method.

SCENE 4
Do not start by guaranteeing lower rates. Offer a review of their current setup, reporting, service concerns, and whether a different solution may help.

REVIEW
Knowledge check. What should you ask for after a brief introduction?. Answer: Permission and a convenient next step.

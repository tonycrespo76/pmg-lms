PARTNERS MERCHANT GROUP TRAINING ACADEMY
The PMG Sales Pitch

INTRO
Welcome to Partners Merchant Group Training Academy. Today: The PMG Sales Pitch.

SCENE 1
A helpful pitch explains how the proposed service addresses the merchant’s real needs: reliable checkout, clear reporting, useful equipment, and support when issues arise.

SCENE 2
Partners Merchant Group helps businesses accept payments and select point-of-sale solutions. We review your current setup, explain your options, and help coordinate onboarding and support.

SCENE 3
Demonstrate a simple checkout, a sales report, or a feature relevant to the business. Use approved demo devices and sample data; never expose a real customer’s payment information.

SCENE 4
Offer a statement review, product demonstration, or written proposal. Confirm the decision maker and follow-up date before leaving.

REVIEW
Knowledge check. What should a strong pitch focus on?. Answer: The merchant’s needs, not a generic feature list.

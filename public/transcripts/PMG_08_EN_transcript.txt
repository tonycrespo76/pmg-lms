Discovering Merchant Needs

INTRO
Welcome to Partners Merchant Group Training Academy. Today: Discovering Merchant Needs.

SCENE 1
How are orders entered? Are there tables, delivery channels, tips, split payments, or multiple locations? Watch the work before suggesting a system.

SCENE 2
What slows checkout? Are reports difficult to use? Is support difficult to reach? What happens when equipment or internet fails?

SCENE 3
Record must-have features, existing integrations, internet connectivity, printer needs, employee permissions, and training language. Check compatibility rather than assuming it.

SCENE 4
Summarize the three most important needs in the merchant’s own words. Then match solutions to those needs, costs, and available support.

REVIEW
Knowledge check. What comes before recommending equipment?. Answer: Understanding the business workflow.

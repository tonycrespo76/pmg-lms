Comparing Statements and Proposals

INTRO
Welcome to Partners Merchant Group Training Academy. Today: Comparing Statements and Proposals.

SCENE 1
Ask the authorized business representative for a recent statement using an approved secure channel. Redact unnecessary account and personal identifiers.

SCENE 2
Compare the same volume and transaction mix. List interchange, assessments, markup, and separate monthly services when present.

SCENE 3
A new POS, software add-on, leasing agreement, or funding feature can change the total cost. Show both recurring and one-time charges.

SCENE 4
Walk through the numbers slowly. Identify what has been verified, what needs underwriting, and what depends on card mix or future volume.

REVIEW
Knowledge check. Should projected savings be treated as guaranteed?. Answer: No. Explain the assumptions.

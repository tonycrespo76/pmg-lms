PARTNERS MERCHANT GROUP TRAINING ACADEMY
Handling Objections

INTRO
Welcome to Partners Merchant Group Training Academy. Today: Handling Objections.

SCENE 1
When the owner says the rate is too high, ask whether the concern is overall cost, equipment price, contract terms, or a previous bad experience.

SCENE 2
You might say: That makes sense. Let’s review the full monthly cost and see whether the proposal addresses the issue. Avoid criticizing the merchant’s current provider.

SCENE 3
Show approved examples, written terms, and relevant product demonstrations. If a requested feature is unavailable, say so and suggest an appropriate alternative.

SCENE 4
Do not pressure the merchant. Offer a follow-up, trial demonstration, or side-by-side comparison that helps them decide.

REVIEW
Knowledge check. What is the first step in handling an objection?. Answer: Ask and listen.

Closing and Submitting an Application

INTRO
Welcome to Partners Merchant Group Training Academy. Today: Closing and Submitting an Application.

SCENE 1
Review the provider, pricing schedule, equipment, software, estimated installation date, cancellation terms, and each fee before asking for a signature.

SCENE 2
Only collect business and identity information through authorized applications and secure systems. Never photograph card data or ask a merchant to send sensitive information through an insecure chat.

SCENE 3
Submitting an application does not guarantee approval or pricing. A processor or acquiring partner may ask for supporting documents or additional verification.

SCENE 4
Document the merchant’s needs, agreed equipment, installation contacts, menu or catalog requirements, training language, and unresolved questions for onboarding.

REVIEW
Knowledge check. Does an application submission guarantee approval?. Answer: No.

POS Systems Versus Terminals

INTRO
Welcome to Partners Merchant Group Training Academy. Today: POS Systems Versus Terminals.

SCENE 1
A payment terminal focuses on accepting and processing payments. Features vary by model, connection method, software, and provider setup.

SCENE 2
A point-of-sale system may combine payments with products, orders, employee permissions, reporting, receipts, and optional business software.

SCENE 3
A simple mobile merchant may need a compact payment solution. A busy restaurant may need tables, kitchen routing, multiple printers, and detailed employee permissions.

SCENE 4
Confirm networking, hardware compatibility, payment integrations, subscriptions, and support coverage before promising a feature.

REVIEW
Knowledge check. Does every terminal provide full restaurant management?. Answer: No.

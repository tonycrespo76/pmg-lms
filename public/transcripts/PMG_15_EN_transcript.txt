Matching Equipment to Merchants

INTRO
Welcome to Partners Merchant Group Training Academy. Today: Matching Equipment to Merchants.

SCENE 1
Consider countertop versus mobile service, indoor versus outdoor use, number of users, internet options, and available electrical outlets.

SCENE 2
List terminals, customer displays, printers, kitchen displays, scanners, cash drawers, and required software. Test how the pieces communicate.

SCENE 3
Include hardware purchase or rental, subscriptions, accessories, installation, support, replacements, and any minimum term or cancellation conditions.

SCENE 4
Confirm shipping, equipment serial numbers, internet readiness, menu setup, payment activation, employee training, and a backup contact.

REVIEW
Knowledge check. Should equipment be chosen before learning the workflow?. Answer: No.

PARTNERS MERCHANT GROUP TRAINING ACADEMY
Merchant Onboarding and Activation

INTRO
Welcome to Partners Merchant Group Training Academy. Today: Merchant Onboarding and Activation.

SCENE 1
Confirm approved account status, legal business details, contacts, pricing acceptance, selected equipment, delivery address, and software requirements.

SCENE 2
Prepare items, taxes, printers, tips, employees, access levels, and integrations as appropriate. Ask the merchant to approve configuration details.

SCENE 3
Use the approved test method to verify the payment flow, receipts, batching, reporting, and any restaurant routing. Do not run live transactions casually to test.

SCENE 4
Identify the primary contact, emergency support number, training completion, unresolved issues, and the first follow-up date.

REVIEW
Knowledge check. When is the installation complete?. Answer: After verified operation and merchant handoff.

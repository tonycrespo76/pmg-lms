Batches, Deposits and Reconciliation

INTRO
Welcome to Partners Merchant Group Training Academy. Today: Batches, Deposits and Reconciliation.

SCENE 1
A batch groups transactions for processing or settlement, depending on the platform. Automatic and manual cutoff behaviors differ, so check the merchant’s actual setup.

SCENE 2
Use transaction history, batch reports, settlement reports, and the merchant’s bank records. The sale date, batch date, and bank posting date may differ.

SCENE 3
Start with gross card sales, then account for refunds, reversals, chargebacks, fees when deducted from funding, and timing differences.

SCENE 4
Confirm which transactions are affected, account status, batch totals, and processor reference numbers. Do not guarantee a funding date without confirmation.

REVIEW
Knowledge check. Are transaction and bank posting dates always identical?. Answer: No.

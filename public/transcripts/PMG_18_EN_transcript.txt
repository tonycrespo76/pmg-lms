Declines and Terminal Troubleshooting

INTRO
Welcome to Partners Merchant Group Training Academy. Today: Declines and Terminal Troubleshooting.

SCENE 1
A decline can come from an issuer decision or a payment system issue. A frozen screen, offline terminal, or failed connection requires a different troubleshooting path.

SCENE 2
Confirm power, network, correct merchant location, device status, and visible error messages. Never ask the customer to read their full card number aloud.

SCENE 3
Before repeating a questionable payment, inspect transaction history or obtain processor guidance. A timeout may not mean the authorization failed.

SCENE 4
Provide model, exact error, connectivity status, time, amount if relevant, and steps attempted. Never include sensitive authentication data in a support ticket.

REVIEW
Knowledge check. Should you immediately retry after a timeout?. Answer: Not until you check for a possible prior approval.

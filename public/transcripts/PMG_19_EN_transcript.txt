Refunds, Voids and Disputes

INTRO
Welcome to Partners Merchant Group Training Academy. Today: Refunds, Voids and Disputes.

SCENE 1
A void typically cancels an eligible transaction before settlement. A refund returns money through a later transaction. A chargeback is a dispute handled through the card ecosystem.

SCENE 2
Eligible actions, time limits, and permissions depend on the processor and POS. Verify the original transaction, amount, and merchant authorization before acting.

SCENE 3
Record the reason code, response deadline, transaction documentation, and delivery or service evidence when relevant. The acquiring partner provides the actual response process.

SCENE 4
Explain that a well-documented response can help but does not guarantee a favorable outcome. Avoid sharing customer data in unauthorized systems.

REVIEW
Knowledge check. Is a refund the same as a chargeback?. Answer: No.

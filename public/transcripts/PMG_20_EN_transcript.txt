Merchant Service and Retention

INTRO
Welcome to Partners Merchant Group Training Academy. Today: Merchant Service and Retention.

SCENE 1
Start by listening, confirming the business impact, and opening a properly documented case. State what you know and what must be investigated.

SCENE 2
A complete payment outage or security concern deserves prompt escalation. Routine report questions and feature requests can use a scheduled follow-up.

SCENE 3
Share an accountable next step and an approved contact path. Never invent technician availability, fee refunds, or a resolution deadline.

SCENE 4
Review common complaints, training gaps, hardware failures, and product fit. A periodic business review can identify opportunities to improve service.

REVIEW
Knowledge check. What should a service representative communicate?. Answer: A verified status and a clear next step.

PCI Data Security Basics

INTRO
Welcome to Partners Merchant Group Training Academy. Today: PCI Data Security Basics.

SCENE 1
Use approved devices, encrypted channels, unique employee access, and secure payment workflows. Limit access to people who genuinely need it.

SCENE 2
PCI DSS prohibits retaining sensitive authentication data such as card verification codes and PIN data after authorization, even if encrypted. Do not keep these in notes or screenshots.

SCENE 3
Never ask for an employee password, full card number, PIN, or verification code to diagnose a problem. Follow approved identity verification and secure escalation procedures.

SCENE 4
If payment data may have been exposed, stop the unsafe activity and notify authorized security or processor contacts according to the incident response procedure.

REVIEW
Knowledge check. May a merchant save card security codes after authorization?. Answer: No.

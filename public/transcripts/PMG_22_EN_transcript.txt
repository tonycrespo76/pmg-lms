Card Rules and Ethical Selling

INTRO
Welcome to Partners Merchant Group Training Academy. Today: Card Rules and Ethical Selling.

SCENE 1
Explain all material fees, conditions, equipment ownership terms, and limitations. Do not promise zero cost, no risk, guaranteed savings, or features not validated.

SCENE 2
Credit surcharges, dual pricing, receipt labels, and consumer notices have different rule requirements. Never apply a credit surcharge to debit or prepaid cards.

SCENE 3
Use authorized systems for applications, statements, and support. Explain why documents are requested and limit unnecessary information collection.

SCENE 4
Laws and network programs can change. Escalate unclear terms, regional restrictions, or a merchant’s custom request to PMG compliance and the appropriate provider.

REVIEW
Knowledge check. Can a representative invent a pricing notice?. Answer: No. Use approved disclosures.

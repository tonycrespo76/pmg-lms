SCENE 1
A restaurant owner complains about slow checkout and high monthly costs. Ask discovery questions, identify reporting and kitchen needs, and request a secure statement review.

SCENE 2
Explain a pricing option without promising exact savings or misrepresenting debit card rules. Review all recurring costs and clearly identify what still needs approval.

SCENE 3
A terminal reports a timeout and the merchant wants to retry immediately. First verify whether the transaction was approved, collect safe diagnostic details, and avoid duplicate charges.

SCENE 4
Your manager should observe a role-play, review your statement analysis, and test your support handoff. This training video alone does not certify someone for live operations.
